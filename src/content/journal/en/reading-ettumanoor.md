The large figures are what everyone comes for, and they deserve it. But they are
finished work, and finished work hides its making.

## Read the edges

Go to where a register meets a doorway, or where a wall has been repaired. That
is where you see the yellow underdrawing still showing through a thin patch of
colour, or a black line that was laid down slightly off and left. You learn more
about how a mural is built from ten minutes at a damaged corner than from an hour
in front of the best figure in the room.
