People sometimes ask whether painting in acrylic makes the work less traditional.
It is a fair question and I think the answer is no, but it is worth saying why.

## What the five colours actually are

Panchavarna is usually explained as a list of materials: shell lime, yellow ochre,
red ochre, a leaf green, lamp soot. But the material was never the point. The
point is that a figure of serene temperament is green, one of power is red, and
the closing line is black. Those are decisions about who someone is, and they hold
whatever the colour is made from.

## What changes and what does not

Acrylic dries faster, which changes how you plan a session, and it sits on canvas
rather than sinking into lime. The proportions do not change. The order of working
does not change. The line still goes on last and still cannot be corrected.
